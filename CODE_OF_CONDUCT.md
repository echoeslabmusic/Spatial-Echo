# Code of Conduct

This is a small project maintained in spare time. Everyone who takes part in
it, in issues, pull requests, or discussions, is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep it on topic and assume good faith.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and that
  some requests will be declined.

Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not acceptable
anywhere in this project. Reports can be sent to hello@echoeslabmusic.com;
they will be read and handled privately. Anyone who repeatedly ignores these
expectations may be blocked from participating.
