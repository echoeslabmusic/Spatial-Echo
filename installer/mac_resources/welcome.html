Spatial Echo

Tape echo, reverb, and spatial processing

This installer will guide you through installing Spatial Echo on your Mac.

You can choose which components to install:

  • VST3 Plugin: for DAWs supporting VST3
  • Audio Unit (AU): for Logic Pro, GarageBand, and other AU hosts
  • Standalone Application
